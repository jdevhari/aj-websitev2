**CampusLedger** is a modular college administration and campus operations platform built for higher-education institutions, colleges, and training institutes. It unifies admissions, student records, academic scheduling, fee tracking, and institutional reporting into a single web-based system enhanced by **multi-agent GenAI orchestration**.

## Core Capabilities

* **Admissions & Enrollment Management:** Manage inquiry pipelines, online applications, document checklists, seat allocation, and enrollment records.
* **Student Information System (SIS):** Maintain centralized academic profiles, department assignments, attendance logs, disciplinary records, and alumni archives.
* **Academics, Timetables & Examinations:** Coordinate course registrations, faculty allocation, exam schedules, gradebook entry, and transcript generation.
* **Fee & Administration Workflows:** Track semester fee schedules, scholarship records, receipts, hostel/transport assignments, and role-based staff approvals.

## Multi-Agent GenAI Orchestration

CampusLedger embeds coordinated AI agents with strict institutional data isolation and staff oversight:

* **Admissions Verification Agent:** Checks uploaded transcripts, ID proofs, and eligibility criteria against department rules and flags incomplete submissions for admissions officers.
* **Student & Parent Helpdesk Agent:** Answers routine academic calendar, fee-schedule, and campus-policy inquiries grounded strictly in official institutional handbooks.
* **Academic Advisor Agent:** Reviews attendance and assessment trends to prepare early-intervention summaries for faculty mentors.
* **Institutional Reporting Agent:** Compiles structured drafts for accreditation, statutory audits, and department review reports from verified campus records.

## Institutional Highlights

* Centralize student, faculty, and administrative data across departments
* Reduce repetitive administrative paperwork during peak admissions and exam cycles
* Enforce role-based access control across students, faculty, registrars, and management
