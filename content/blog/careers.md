#### About the Role

We are looking for a **Senior Software AI Engineer** to design, build, and ship production software systems powered by modern AI and LLM capabilities. You will own technical design from prototype to production across enterprise consulting engagements and our internal SaaS platforms.

#### Key Responsibilities

* Architect and build production AI-powered applications, APIs, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines, and tool-using agent workflows.
* Design reliable evaluation suites, guardrails, latency/cost optimizations, and observability for AI services in production.
* Develop clean, maintainable backend services and cloud architectures across AWS, Microsoft Azure, or IBM Cloud.
* Partner directly with engineering leadership and clients to scope technical requirements, run architecture reviews, and deliver iteratively.
* Mentor engineers through code reviews, clear documentation, and pragmatic engineering standards.

#### Qualifications & Experience

* 5+ years of professional software engineering experience shipping production systems.
* Strong proficiency in **Python** and/or **TypeScript / Node.js** (experience with Go or Java is a plus).
* Hands-on experience building with LLM APIs, embeddings, vector search, structured outputs, function/tool calling, and automated evaluations.
* Solid foundation in backend engineering: relational/NoSQL databases, REST/GraphQL APIs, authentication, containers (Docker), and CI/CD pipelines.
* Practical experience deploying and operating workloads on **AWS**, **Microsoft Azure**, or **IBM Cloud**.
* Clear communication skills and comfort working in a small, high-trust, senior engineering environment.
