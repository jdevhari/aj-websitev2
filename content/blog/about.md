**Aerojini Solutions LLP** is an Indian technology consulting and software engineering firm focused on building maintainable, production-grade digital products. We partner with startups and growing organizations to modernize legacy systems, architect cloud infrastructure, and deliver custom web, mobile, analytics, and SaaS platforms.

## What We Do

* **Application Modernization:** Refactoring legacy software into modular, secure, cloud-native applications with automated CI/CD pipelines.
* **Cloud Consulting & Infrastructure:** Architecture, migration, cost optimization, and reliability engineering across AWS, Microsoft Azure, and IBM Cloud.
* **Custom Software & SaaS Engineering:** Full-stack web and mobile application development, API integrations, IoT data pipelines, and applied AI workflows.
* **Product Platforms:** Purpose-built software and multi-agent GenAI solutions including [FlowGrid BPM](/blog/flowgrid-bpm), [CampusLedger](/blog/campusledger), [PayBridge Connect](/blog/paybridge-connect), [CartPulse AI](/blog/cartpulse-ai), [LexBrief Workspace](/blog/lexbrief-workspace), [Memberjini](/blog/memberjini), and [Tradejini](/blog/tradejini).

## How We Work

1. **Discovery & Architecture:** We evaluate business constraints, existing infrastructure, security requirements, and delivery milestones before writing production code.
2. **Iterative Delivery:** Work is broken into transparent milestones with regular demonstrations, version-controlled repositories, and automated builds.
3. **Handover & Continuity:** Every engagement emphasizes clean documentation, operational visibility, and long-term maintainability for your internal team.
