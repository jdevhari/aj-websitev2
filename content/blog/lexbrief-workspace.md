**LexBrief Workspace** is a legal operations and document-intelligence platform designed for in-house legal teams, compliance officers, and law firms. It organizes contracts, matter dossiers, obligation timelines, and internal legal playbooks in a secure workspace powered by **citation-grounded multi-agent GenAI orchestration**.

## Core Capabilities

* **Centralized Contract & Matter Repository:** Organize agreements, amendments, notices, and supporting documents with structured metadata and version history.
* **Clause Library & Playbook Management:** Maintain approved fallback clauses, negotiation positions, and jurisdiction-specific review checklists.
* **Obligation & Renewal Tracking:** Track notice periods, renewal dates, milestone deliverables, and compliance deadlines across active contracts.
* **Tenant Isolation & Audit Trails:** Enforce strict matter-level access controls, document permissions, and full review histories for internal governance.

## Multi-Agent GenAI Orchestration

LexBrief Workspace orchestrates narrow, auditable agents with mandatory citation grounding and lawyer-in-the-loop review:

* **Clause Extraction Agent:** Identifies and indexes key commercial and legal provisions (indemnity, limitation of liability, termination, governing law, IP ownership) linked directly to exact page and paragraph citations.
* **Playbook Comparison Agent:** Compares incoming third-party drafts against the organization's approved playbook and highlights deviations and missing standard clauses.
* **Document Synthesis Agent:** Produces structured executive summaries, amendment diffs, and chronology briefs across multi-document matter folders—strictly grounded in uploaded files.
* **Deadline & Obligation Agent:** Extracts contractual dates, renewal windows, and recurring obligations into structured calendar reminders for legal-ops verification.

## Legal-Ops Highlights

* Accelerate first-pass contract triage while keeping every finding traceable to source text
* Protect sensitive matter files with strict tenant boundaries and PII-aware handling
* Designed around mandatory human review so qualified counsel retains final decision authority
