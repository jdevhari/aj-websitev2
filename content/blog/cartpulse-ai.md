**CartPulse AI** is a Shopify operations and catalog-intelligence application built for growing D2C and multi-SKU brands. Connected directly through official Shopify Admin APIs and webhooks, it streamlines product catalog maintenance, inventory monitoring, fulfillment exception workflows, and storefront insights using **multi-agent GenAI orchestration**.

## Core Capabilities

* **Catalog & Metafield Management:** Standardize product titles, attributes, SEO metadata, variant tags, and structured Shopify metafields across large catalogs.
* **Inventory & Fulfillment Monitoring:** Track low-stock thresholds, delayed fulfillments, and unshipped orders across locations with automated team alerts.
* **Order Operations Workflows:** Surface address issues, partial shipments, return requests, and high-priority orders in one merchant dashboard.
* **Merchandising & Store Analytics:** Consolidate SKU velocity, return-reason patterns, and collection performance without manual spreadsheet exports.

## Multi-Agent GenAI Orchestration

CartPulse AI coordinates specialized commerce agents grounded directly in live Shopify store data:

* **Catalog Enrichment Agent:** Audits incomplete product listings, normalizes structured attributes, and drafts consistent descriptions and tags for merchant review before publishing.
* **Inventory & Restock Advisor Agent:** Analyzes historical SKU movement alongside current stock levels to highlight reorder priorities and slow-moving inventory.
* **Order Support Triage Agent:** Uses read-only Shopify order and shipment lookups to draft accurate status updates and escalate fulfillment exceptions to store staff.
* **Merchandising Insights Agent:** Summarizes weekly trends across collections, refund reasons, and bundle opportunities in plain-language briefs for store operators.

## Merchant Highlights

* Built on Shopify's official OAuth, Admin API, and webhook architecture
* Keeps merchants in control with draft previews and approval gates before live store updates
* Reduces repetitive catalog and order-operations work as SKU counts scale
