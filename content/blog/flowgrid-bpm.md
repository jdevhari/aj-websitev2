**FlowGrid BPM** is a business process management and workflow automation platform designed for operations, finance, and enterprise IT teams. It combines deterministic BPMN-style process engines with bounded **multi-agent GenAI orchestration** to handle both structured approvals and document-heavy exceptions.

## Core Capabilities

* **Visual Process Modeling & State Management:** Define multi-stage approval workflows, conditional routing, escalation timers, and SLA tracking across departments.
* **System & ERP Connectors:** Integrate cleanly with existing CRMs, accounting software, ticketing tools, and internal REST/GraphQL APIs.
* **Auditability & Role-Based Governance:** Maintain immutable execution logs, role-based permissions, and versioned workflow definitions for internal compliance reviews.
* **Exception Handling & Human-in-the-Loop Gates:** Automatically pause workflows for supervisor approval whenever thresholds, policy checks, or confidence scores require manual review.

## Multi-Agent GenAI Orchestration

FlowGrid BPM coordinates specialized AI agents inside deterministic workflow boundaries:

* **Document Intake Agent:** Extracts structured fields from invoices, purchase orders, vendor forms, and PDFs into typed workflow payloads.
* **Policy & Compliance Validation Agent:** Cross-checks extracted records against internal SOPs, approval matrices, and master data before advancing a stage.
* **Exception Triage Agent:** Summarizes missing fields, discrepancies, or blocked steps and routes concise decision briefs to the right approver.
* **Process Analytics Agent:** Monitors cycle-time bottlenecks across workflow runs and surfaces actionable operational summaries for process owners.

## Operational Highlights

* Reduce manual handoffs across cross-functional approval processes
* Keep deterministic business rules in code while using GenAI strictly for unstructured inputs and synthesis
* Retain full trace logs for every automated step and human approval
